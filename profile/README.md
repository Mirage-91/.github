# Graz BCI Racing Team MIRAGE91 🧠🏁

We are a student team at **TU Graz** that builds brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) and races them in competitions such as the **Cybathlon** and the **Cortical Peaks Challenge 2026**. Our pilots control games with their thoughts, and our job is to turn their EEG signals into reliable commands.

## What we work on

| Repository | What's inside |
|---|---|
| [**Mirage91**](https://github.com/Mirage-91/Mirage91) | Main code: offline/online EEG pipelines, classifiers, game connectors, how-to notebooks (MI, P300) |
| [**Mirage-91.github.io**](https://github.com/Mirage-91/Mirage-91.github.io) | Our website, [mirage-91.github.io](https://mirage-91.github.io) |
| [**project-template**](https://github.com/Mirage-91/project-template) | Starting point for new projects. Click *Use this template* |

## Teams

- **Admins**: organization, onboarding, repo access
- **Signal Processing & ML**: preprocessing, features, classifiers
- **Software & Game Interface**: online pipeline, streaming, game connectors
- **Hardware & EEG**: amplifiers, electrodes, recording setup
- **Outreach & Website**: website, social media, sponsors
